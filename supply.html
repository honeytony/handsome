Primary focus

Secondary industrial
metal materials.

Metal-bearing residues and by-products recovered from industrial processes. Turnings and shavings from machining, off-cuts and drops from fabrication, mixed lots from workshops and plants. Collected, consolidated and returned into the melting and refining chain instead of being written off.

Forms

Shavings, turnings, off-cuts, drops, mixed lots

Preparation

Sorted and consolidated through third-party facilities

Origin

Machining shops, fabricators, industrial plants

Destination

Smelters, refiners and foundries
