Our position

Material has a second life.

We handle the part in between.

Every tonne of industrial residue that goes back into a furnace is a tonne that never has to be mined. That is the business, and it only works if the material actually arrives.

Supply begins in the Gulf.

We buy from producers, workshops and stockists across the GCC, where we hold the relationships directly. A shorter chain means fewer places for an order to go quiet.

We are the link, not a layer.

Contracts sit with us on both sides. One counterparty, one point of accountability — from purchase order through to unloading.

Specification is not negotiable.

Grade, composition and tolerance are agreed before anything ships, and the documents travel with the material.
